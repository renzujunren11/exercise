## 多項分布 $\text{Multi}(n, \bm{p})$

### $\bm{X} = \{X_1, X_2, \dots, X_m\}$ の同時分布  

$$
P(\bm{X} = \bm{x}) = \frac{n!}{x_1!x_2!\cdots x_m!}p_1^{x_1}p_2^{x_2}\cdots p_m^{x_m}
$$
(ただし、$p_1 + p_2 + \cdots + p_m = 1, X_1 + X_2 + \cdots + X_m = n$)

### $X_i, X_j$ ($i\neq j$) の同時分布

$$
P(X_i = x_i, X_j=x_j) = \frac{n!}{x_i!x_j!(n-x_i-x_j)!}p_i^{x_i}p_j^{x_j}(1-p_i-p_j)^{n-x_i-x_j}
$$

### $X_i, X_j$ ($i\neq j$) の共分散

$$
\begin{align*}
E[X_iX_j] &= \sum_{x_i=0}^n\sum_{x_j=0}^{n-x_i}x_ix_jP(X_i = x_i, X_j=x_j)\\
& (第一の総和で、x_i=n のときx_j=0 なので項がなくなる)\\
&= \sum_{x_i=0}^{n-1}\sum_{x_j=0}^{n-x_i}x_ix_jP(X_i = x_i, X_j=x_j)\\
&= \sum_{x_i=0}^{n-1}\sum_{x_j=0}^{n-x_i}x_ix_j\frac{n!}{x_i!x_j!(n-x_i-x_j)!}p_i^{x_i}p_j^{x_j}(1-p_i-p_j)^{n-x_i-x_j}\\
&= \sum_{x_i=0}^{n-1}\frac{n!}{x_i!}x_ip_i^{x_i}\sum_{x_j=1}^{n-x_i}\frac{1}{(x_j-1)!(n-x_i-x_j)!}p_j^{x_j}(1-p_i-p_j)^{n-x_i-x_j}\\
& (s = x_j-1 とおく)\\
&= \sum_{x_i=0}^{n-1}\frac{n!}{x_i!}x_ip_i^{x_i}\sum_{s=0}^{n-1-x_i}\frac{1}{s!(n-1-x_i-s)!}p_j^{s+1}(1-p_i-p_j)^{n-1-x_i-s}\\
&= \sum_{x_i=0}^{n-1}\frac{n!}{x_i!(n-1-x_i)!}x_ip_i^{x_i}p_j\sum_{s=0}^{n-1-x_i}\frac{(n-1-x_i)!}{s!(n-1-x_i-s)!}p_j^{s}(1-p_i-p_j)^{n-1-x_i-s}\\
& (二項定理を使う)\\
&= \sum_{x_i=0}^{n-1}\frac{n!}{x_i!(n-1-x_i)!}x_ip_i^{x_i}p_j(1-p_i)^{n-1-x_i}\\
&= p_j\sum_{x_i=1}^{n-1}\frac{n!}{(x_i-1)!(n-1-x_i)!}p_i^{x_i}(1-p_i)^{n-1-x_i}\\
& (t = x_i - 1 とおく)\\
&= p_j\sum_{t=0}^{n-2}\frac{n!}{t!(n-2-t)!}p_i^{s+1}(1-p_i)^{n-2-t}\\
&= n(n-1)p_ip_j\sum_{t=0}^{n-2}\frac{(n-2)!}{t!(n-2-t)!}p_i^{s}(1-p_i)^{n-2-t}\\
& (二項定理を使う)\\
&= n(n-1)p_ip_j
\end{align*}
$$

$E[X_i] = np_i$ なので、

$$
\begin{align*}
Cov(X_i, X_j) &= E[X_iX_j] - E[X_i]E[X_j]\\
&= n(n-1)p_ip_j - n^2p_ip_j\\
&= -np_ip_j
\end{align*}
$$
