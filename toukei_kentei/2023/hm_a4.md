## [3]

回帰係数 $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$
$$
\begin{align*}
a &= \frac{Cov(x, y)}{V[x]}\\
b &= E[y] - \frac{Cov(x, y)}{V[x]}E[x]\\
\\
c &= E[x] - \frac{Cov(x, y)}{V[y]}E[y]\\
d &= \frac{Cov(x, y)}{V[y]}
\end{align*}
$$

男性
$$
\begin{align*}
E[x] &= \bar{x_M} = 7.0\\
E[y] &= \bar{y_M} = 8.4\
\\
V[x] &= \sum_x (x - \bar{x_M})^2p_x\\
&= (5-7)^2*0.1 + (6-7.0)^2*0.15 + (7-7.0)^2*0.4 + (8-7.0)^2*0.35\\
&=0.9\\
\\
V[\hat{y}] &= \sum_y (y - \bar{y_M})^2p_y\\
&= (7.2-8.4)^2*0.1 + (7.8-8.4)^2*0.15 + (8.4-8.4)^2*0.4 + (9.0-8.4)^2*0.35\\
&=0.324\\
V[y] &= V[\hat{y}] + s^2\\
&= 0.324 + 0.972\\
&= 1.\\
Cov(x, y) &= aV[x]\\
&= 0.6*0.9\\
&=0.54\\
\\
c &= 7.0 - \frac{0.54}{1.296} * 8.4 = 3.5\\
d &= \frac{0.54}{1.296} = 0.4167
\end{align*}
$$
