負の二項分布 $\text{NBin}(m, p)$ の確率関数
$$
\begin{align*}
P(X=x) &= \binom{m+x-1}{x}p^m(1-p)^x\\
&= \frac{(m+x-1)!}{x!(m-1)!}p^m(1-p)^x
\end{align*}
$$
負の二項分布の期待値
$$
\begin{align*}
E[X] &= \sum_{i=0}^{\infty}x\cdot\frac{(m+x-1)!}{x!(m-1)!}p^m(1-p)^x\\
&= \sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\frac{(m+x-1)!}{(x-1)!(m-1)!}p^m(1-p)^x\\
&= \sum_{i=1}^{\infty}\frac{m(1-p)}{p}\cdot\frac{(m+x-1)!}{(x-1)!m!}p^{m+1}(1-p)^{x-1}\\
\end{align*}
$$
$y=x-1$, $n=m+1$ とおけば、
$$
\begin{align*}
E[X] &= \frac{m(1-p)}{p}\cdot\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\frac{(n+y-1)!}{y!(n-1)!}p^{n}(1-p)^{y}\\
&= \frac{m(1-p)}{p}
\end{align*}
$$
負の二項分布の分散
$$
\begin{align*}
E[X^2] &= \sum_{i=0}^{\infty}x^2\cdot\frac{(m+x-1)!}{x!(m-1)!}p^m(1-p)^x\\
&= \sum_{i=0}^{\infty}x\cdot\frac{(m+x-1)!}{(x-1)!(m-1)!}p^m(1-p)^x\\
&= \sum_{i=1}^{\infty}x\cdot\frac{m(1-p)}{p}\cdot\frac{(m+x-1)!}{(x-1)!m!}p^{m+1}(1-p)^{x-1}\\
\end{align*}
$$
$y=x-1$, $n=m+1$ とおけば、
$$
\begin{align*}
E[X^2] &= \frac{m(1-p)}{p}\cdot\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}(y+1)\cdot\frac{(n+y-1)!}{y!(n-1)!}p^{n}(1-p)^{y}\\
&= \frac{m(1-p)}{p}\left(\frac{n(1-p)}{p} + 1\right)\\
&= \frac{m(1-p)}{p}\left(\frac{(m+1)(1-p)}{p} + 1\right)\\
\\
V[X] &= E[X^2] - E[X]^2\\
&= \frac{m(1-p)}{p}\left(\frac{(m+1)(1-p)}{p} + 1\right) - \frac{m^2(1-p)^2}{p^2}\\
&= \frac{m(1-p)}{p^2}
\end{align*}
$$

$X/m$ の収束先
$$
E[(X/m - E[X/m])^2] = V[X/m] = \frac{1}{m}\frac{1-p}{p^2} \rightarrow 0
$$

中心極限定理より以下が$N(0, 1)$ に漸近する
$$
\frac{\sqrt{X/m - E[X/m]}}{\sqrt{V[X/m]}} = \frac{(X-E[X])/\sqrt{m}}{\sqrt{V[X/m]}}
$$
したがって、$(X-E[X])/\sqrt{m}$ は、N(0, V[X/m]) に漸近する
